# Motion graphics

Prefer HyperFrames when installed. Build deterministic, seekable animation timelines and keep text readable at delivery resolution. Suitable techniques include HTML/CSS, GSAP, SVG, Lottie, Three.js/WebGL and canvas when supported by the installed HyperFrames workflow.

Use `/asset-agent` for missing imagery, textures or vectors. Prefer Iconify/open vectors for symbolic graphics. Render locally and verify duration, FPS, dimensions, codecs and audio sync.
